My game idea is to make a point-and-click puzzle/escape room game. The player is in a locked room and views one side of the room at once from the center of the room or navigates a couple of different rooms (tbd). There will be different clues to solve puzzles and enter codes. To interact with things the player clicks on items and certain spots. The goal of the game is to find cat food and feed the cats, after which the player can escape the room.
